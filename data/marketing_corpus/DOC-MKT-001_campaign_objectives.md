## 1. Overview and Core Objectives
This document sets the strategic objectives for enterprise B2B marketing initiatives in Q4. All campaign proposals must align with core corporate growth goals. Note: This document is a synthetic demonstration specification for placement testing decision-support workflows.

The primary objective for Q4 is driving a 25% increase in sales-qualified enterprise leads (SQLs). All messaging must emphasize enterprise security, verified data grounding, and high-reliability operational tooling.

## 2. Approved Advertising Channels
Campaign proposals must allocate spend across the following approved core acquisition channels:
1. **Paid Search:** Google Ads targeting high-intent keywords relating to retrieval systems and enterprise AI tooling.
2. **Paid Social:** LinkedIn Sponsored Content targeting Engineering Managers, VP of Engineering, and Chief Technology Officers.
3. **Content Marketing:** Technical whitepapers, case studies, and engineering architecture briefs.
4. **Email Nurture:** Segmented opt-in email campaigns targeting existing technical subscribers.

Proposals seeking to utilize unlisted channels (such as TikTok, radio broadcasting, or print billboards) require prior authorization and are currently categorized as unsupported channels.
