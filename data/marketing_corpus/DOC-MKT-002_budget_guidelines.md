## 1. Budget Ceilings and Financial Limits
This document defines binding budgetary guardrails for all Q4 campaign submissions. Synthetic demonstration policy for testing.

The absolute spending ceiling for any single Q4 campaign proposal is $50,000 USD. Any proposal requesting total funds exceeding $50,000 must be rejected immediately during validation.

A mandatory 15% contingency reserve ($7,500 on a $50,000 budget) must remain unallocated to absorb ad auction price volatility. Consequently, active planned media spend cannot exceed $42,500.

## 2. Channel Budget Caps
Planned expenditures must conform to channel-specific sub-allocations:
- **Paid Search (Google Ads):** Capped at a maximum allocation of $20,000.
- **Paid Social (LinkedIn Ads):** Capped at a maximum allocation of $15,000.
- **Influencer and KOL Partnerships:** Allocated spend ceiling is set to $25,000.
- **Content Production:** Capped at $7,500.

All proposed budgets must be backed by unit economics and itemized channel estimates.
