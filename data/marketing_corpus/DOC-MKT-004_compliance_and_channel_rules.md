## 1. Compliance and Data Privacy Mandates
All marketing initiatives must strictly adhere to enterprise data protection policies and brand safety guidelines. Synthetic demonstration compliance policy.

Any digital campaign collecting prospective customer contact data must include an unbundled, affirmative opt-in consent checkbox. Pre-checked consent boxes are strictly prohibited. Privacy disclosures must reference enterprise retention policies.

## 2. Channel Safety and Influencer Restrictions
To protect corporate brand reputation and mitigate third-party risk:
- **Influencer and KOL Partnerships:** Maximum allowable spend is strictly capped at $10,000 per campaign to prevent excessive budget concentration. Any engagement requires legal review of influencer background history.
- **Competitor Trademark Bidding:** Prohibited across all paid search ad networks.
- **Mandatory Claims Substantiation:** Any claim asserting uptime, benchmark performance, or compliance certifications must cite verifiable public documentation.
