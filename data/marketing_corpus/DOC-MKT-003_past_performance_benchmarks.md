## 1. Past Campaign Metrics
This document summarizes verified historical performance benchmarks from Q1-Q3 marketing initiatives. Synthetic demonstration data for workflow evaluation.

Across preceding quarters, enterprise acquisition campaigns yielded the following verified baseline metrics:
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR):** Average baseline across paid search and social is 2.4%.
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Historical average CAC is $32.00 per qualified lead, with an acceptable operational threshold of up to $35.00.
- **Landing Page Conversion Rate:** 4.1% from unique visitor to demo request.
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** Target baseline ROAS is 2.8x over a standard 90-day pipeline maturation window.

## 2. Gaps and Unverified Media
Historical performance data is strictly limited to Google Search, LinkedIn Ads, and technical whitepaper syndication. 

No corporate performance data, conversion benchmarks, or audience research exists for consumer video platforms (e.g. TikTok, Instagram Reels), linear television broadcasting, or print magazines. Proposals projecting returns on unverified media must note this as an unknown factor.
