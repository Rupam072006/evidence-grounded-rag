## 1. Scope and Policy Objectives
This policy defines mandatory financial controls, budget ceilings, and spending guidelines for all corporate marketing campaigns, growth initiatives, and promotional activities. All campaign proposals submitted for evaluation must comply with the spending caps and reserve thresholds outlined in this document.

## 2. Campaign Budget Ceilings
The following budgetary boundaries are strictly enforced:
- **Maximum Campaign Budget:** The maximum allowable budget allocation for any individual marketing campaign or initiative is **$50,000.00**. Any proposal requesting more than $50,000.00 is non-compliant and must be rejected during financial verification.
- **Mandatory Contingency Reserve:** Every approved campaign must preserve a minimum 15% reserve buffer ($7,500.00 on a $50,000.00 ceiling) to mitigate unexpected advertising auction volatility, cost per click increases, or creative re-budgeting.
- **Active Spend Limit:** Proposals allocating more than 85% of the total budget ($42,500.00) without explicit executive waiver require conditional approval status.

## 3. Expense Categorization and Allocation Rules
- **Direct Advertising Spend:** Funds allocated to search, social, or programmatic ad networks must not exceed 65% of the campaign's total budget.
- **Agency and Production Fees:** External creative production, video generation, and copywriting fees must remain below 25% of the total campaign budget.
- **Experimental Testing:** A maximum of 10% of campaign funding may be allocated to unproven or exploratory advertising channels.

## 4. Financial Audit and Human Approval Gate
No autonomous software system, algorithmic agent, or pipeline is permitted to disburse funds, execute ad purchase contracts, or bind company capital. All campaign proposals that successfully pass automated evidence verification must be placed into `AWAITING_APPROVAL` status and routed to a designated human finance controller for formal sign-off.
