## 1. Purpose and Governance Restriction
This governance directive enforces heightened financial discipline and risk mitigation on social media partnerships, external agency engagements, and influencer marketing collaborations. 

## 2. Restricted Influencer Spending Limit
Under this restricted governance policy, total expenditure allocated to influencer marketing and creator sponsorships is strictly capped at a ceiling of **$10,000.00** per fiscal quarter.

Any proposed allocation for influencer marketing exceeding $10,000.00 is strictly prohibited and requires a formal executive exception signed by the Chief Financial Officer. 

## 3. Strict Audit Controls
- Creator engagements are capped at a maximum of $2,500.00 per individual partner.
- No single creator partnership may represent more than 25% of the $10,000.00 restricted ceiling.
- All agreements must be audited for brand reputational alignment prior to disbursement.

## 4. Conflict Notice
This document enforces a strict $10,000.00 ceiling, which directly contradicts the $25,000.00 limit stated in the standard policy (DOC-024). When evaluating proposals involving influencer marketing, this contradiction must be highlighted as an unresolved policy conflict.
