## 1. Purpose and Governance
This policy establishes standard operational rules and financial ceilings for influencer partnerships, creator sponsorships, and developer advocate collaborations across marketing programs.

## 2. Standard Influencer Spending Limit
Under standard marketing operating guidelines, the total expenditure allocated to influencer marketing and creator sponsorships is capped at a maximum of **$25,000.00** per fiscal quarter. 

Campaign managers may allocate up to $25,000.00 to individual creator contracts or syndicated brand partnerships provided each creator signs the standard brand safety rider and complies with Federal Trade Commission (FTC) disclosure rules.

## 3. Tiered Creator Allocations
- **Tier 1 (Key Opinion Leaders):** Contracts up to $15,000.00 per engagement.
- **Tier 2 (Niche Technical Creators):** Contracts up to $5,000.00 per engagement.
- **Tier 3 (Micro-influencers):** Contracts up to $1,500.00 per engagement.
- Combined total expenditure across all tiers must not exceed the quarterly ceiling of $25,000.00.
